Three Strategic Moves

Recommendations
Move 01

Reporter-as-Creator & Social Funnel

Fix the handoff from social discovery to BI's owned ecosystem
THE BRIEF — Daily · 60s
Named BI reporter covers top 3 headlines. Opens reporter-on-screen. Closes with a direct link to the full article on BI.
MONEY MOVES — 3× wk · 90s
Personal finance reporter with direct opt-in to Money Memo newsletter.
CAREER INTEL — 2× wk · 2min
Job market reporter + guest expert. Links to Career Dispatch newsletter opt-in.
Survey signal: 44% scroll past after social video — frictionless CTA is the fix.
Move 02

Free Daily Briefing & Newsletter Verticals

Build a daily reading ritual before asking for money
FREE DAILY BRIEFING
60-second personalised briefing by interest area. No paywall. Surface the subscription offer only after 5+ return visits.
MONEY MEMO — Every Monday
Salary negotiation · ETFs & investing basics · Side income tax strategy · Career transition planning. Gap: BI beats Morning Brew on data depth.
CAREER DISPATCH — Every Wednesday
Job market pulse · Skills in demand · Interview prep with insiders. Gap: BI beats LinkedIn on reporting depth.
Pricing: $5–10/mo (48% WTP range) · Employer-billed bundles for the 40% with company budgets.
Move 03

AI as Production Force-Multiplier

Scale output without diluting editorial trust
PHASE 1 · Days 1–30 (10% Pilot)
AI-assisted video transcription, article-to-script conversion, thumbnail A/B testing. Human editors review all output.
PHASE 1 · Days 31–60 (25% Scale)
Newsletter brief generation, SEO headline optimisation, topic trend detection. Measure vs. manual baseline.
PHASE 1 · Days 61–90 (50% Integration)
Full workflow integration with human-in-the-loop sign-off. AI handles scale; reporters retain editorial voice.
Survey signal: 55% still read AI-assisted content when human sign-off is clearly visible.

AI Risks & Guardrails

AI Deployment
84% of respondents say AI-written content erodes trust or willingness to pay — five guardrails make AI safe to deploy
1
No AI bylines
Reporters own every piece. AI is invisible production infrastructure, never a named contributor.
2
Human editorial sign-off before every publish
Every AI-assisted piece requires reporter or editor approval. No exceptions at any pilot stage.
3
AI never sets the editorial agenda
AI surfaces trend signals and suggests topics. Reporters make every news judgment call.
4
Trust metrics monitored weekly
Shares, time on page and subscription rates tracked continuously. Any decline triggers immediate review.
5
Proprietary data isolated from external crawlers
BI's investigative data, source notes and exclusive datasets separated from public AI training pipelines.
DATA VALIDATION
55% of respondents would still read AI-assisted content when human sign-off is clearly visible. Transparency is the guardrail, not the absence of AI.

Key Insights Behind the Strategy

Insight Summary
Insight 01 · Audience Behavior

The discovery is happening. The conversion is not.

Friction at the social-to-owned handoff is the root problem
Discover BI first on social 49
18–34 pay more attention to creators than brand accounts 51%
Implication: Build a 4-step funnel — Hook via reporter-led video → Bridge to BI article (no paywall first touch) → Engage via daily briefing → Convert via referral + employer bundle.
Insight 02 · Engagement Model

Readers don't cancel due to price. They cancel because BI never became routine.

Not a content problem — a ritual problem
Cancelled because "didn't use enough" 35%
Daily short updates = #1 habit driver 73
The 5-Visit Rule: Surface the subscription offer only after a reader has returned 5+ times. Convert the habitual reader, not just the curious one.
Insight 03 · AI Strategy

AI destroys value as an author and creates value as invisible infrastructure.

The guardrail is transparency, not absence
Say AI bylines reduce trust or willingness to pay 84%
Still read AI content with human sign-off visible 55%
BI's moat: Named reporters, editorial point of view and institutional credibility cannot be replicated by a language model. Protect these at every stage of the AI pilot.
